This week's 5-minute trick: Turn a messy voice note into a neat to-do list

  1. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini on your phone.
  2. Tap the microphone and ramble everything on your mind for a minute.
  3. Type: "Turn that into a to-do list grouped by today, this week and later. Keep each item under 8 words."
  4. Copy the list into your notes app. Done.

5 tools worth a look

  • Gamma: type a topic, get a slide deck in a minute.
  • Perplexity: answers with clickable sources, so you can check facts.
  • Gemini Notebook: upload your notes and get quiz questions or a spoken summary.
  • CapCut: free video editor with templates and auto-captions.
  • Suno: describe a song and it writes and sings it.
